Dashline Car Launcher – Privacy Policy

Effective: 19 November 2026

Dashline is a home-screen app for car head units and tablets. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it has no server of its own. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

Information the app uses, and where it stays

  • Location. With your permission, the app reads your device's location while it is open to show your position, speed and heading on the map. Location is processed only on your device. It is not stored beyond the current session, not sent to us and not shared with anyone. The app does not access location in the background.
  • Media playback information. If you turn on notification access, the app reads which app is playing media and its title, artist, artwork and progress, so it can show them and offer play, pause and skip. This is processed only on your device. The app does not read, store or transmit the contents of your notifications.
  • List of installed apps. To show the All apps screen the app asks Android for apps that can be launched. This list is used on your device only.
  • Settings. Your choices (theme, accent colour, units, hidden apps) are stored on your device.

Network use

The app connects to the internet only when you download or update an offline map region. The request goes to the server that hosts the map files (a file download), which can see your IP address and the file requested, as any web server does. No identifier, location or other personal information is sent by the app. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Sharing and selling

We do not collect, share or sell personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices

You can revoke the location permission or notification access at any time in Android settings, and delete downloaded maps in the app (Settings → Map → Offline maps). Uninstalling the app removes all its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
